# Campus Customs: Design (Problem 10)

**Concept: "Varsity Night."** Black like a night game at the Yale Bowl, hot-pink scoreboard light, chenille varsity patches, and paper hang tags. It keeps the class's black-and-pink style, but every element borrows from real college merch (letterman patches, pennants, ticket stubs, price tags), so the site feels like a campus shop rather than a template.

## What changed and why it helps people stay and buy

| Area | What I changed | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|
| **Fonts** | *Graduate* (collegiate block letters) for labels, prices, sizes, and the logo; *Bricolage Grotesque* for headlines; *Inter* for body text. | Instantly reads as "college apparel" and gives a clear hierarchy: varsity type marks *what to scan* (category, price, size), and Inter stays easy to read. |
| **Color & texture** | Warm off-white text, one hot-pink accent (#ff3f9e), amber only for scarcity, a soft pink "stadium light" glow, and a subtle film-grain overlay. | One accent color means the eye goes straight to calls to action and prices. The grain makes the black feel like fabric, which feels more premium. |
| **Logo & mascot mark** | An SVG **chenille patch** (scalloped edge, dashed stitching, "CC") used as the logo, the chat avatar, and the "ASK" chat launcher. | One memorable brand mark that ties the shop and the assistant together, so the chatbot feels like part of the store and shoppers trust it. |
| **Hero** | Giant outlined **varsity "BULLDOG"** with a two-tone offset layer and a one-time neon flicker on load; yard-line stripes and a slow stadium-light sweep; three products as **floating polaroids with paper price tags**. | Products and prices show above the fold in the first second, and it stays on-brand and memorable. Two clear CTAs: *Shop the collection* and *Ask our assistant*. |
| **Scoreboard ticker** | A pink marquee under the nav (pauses on hover) with true facts only: officially licensed, 57 Broadway, live stock, XS–XXL. | Builds trust without fake sales or shipping promises. |
| **Hierarchy on Home** | Hero → **Shop by category** (circular product tiles with counts) → **Going fast** (live stock meter) → **The classics** → stitched-patch value props. | A clear path from browsing to buying. Each section is one click from the filtered Products page (`/products?category=…`). |
| **Honest urgency** | A "Going fast" rail and card badge only when total stock is **≤ 30 units** (11 of 102 styles), plus an amber **stock meter**. When a size filter is on, the badge says "Only N left in M". | Real scarcity motivates purchase without dark patterns, and it matches what the assistant says. |
| **Product cards** | **Paper hang tag** price that swings on hover; a **3D tilt** that follows the cursor; image zoom; size pills (sold-out struck through, searched size filled pink); "View details →" slides in; **staggered entrance** animation. | Feels like picking a shirt off a table. Price and size availability can be judged without opening the item, which cuts wasted clicks. |
| **Product page** | Breadcrumb (Home / Products / Hoodies / …); **cursor-following 1.9× zoom** to inspect prints; a large hang-tag price; **color swatch dots**; sizes as **notched ticket stubs** (amber "Only 2 left", struck-through sold out); trust row; "Ask about this item"; **"You may also like"** (same category, in stock). | Answers the purchase questions (what it looks like up close, the price, whether my size is in stock) right on the page, and the related row keeps shoppers browsing instead of leaving. |
| **Buttons** | Pill buttons with a solid "pressable" bottom edge that sinks on click, plus a pink glow on hover. | Calls to action look clickable and give satisfying feedback. |
| **Chat feel** | A patch launcher labeled "Ask Campus Customs" with a soft pulse; the panel springs open from the corner; a pink header with the patch avatar and a status dot; assistant bubbles carry the patch avatar; messages pop in; **bouncing pink typing dots plus the live status** ("Checking stock…"); pill chips with a fade edge; saved-chat dividers in varsity type. | The assistant feels like a friendly shop employee, not a support widget. Visible progress keeps people waiting through the answer instead of closing the chat. |
| **Motion** | Page transitions (fade/slide per route), springy hover on cards, tiles, and logo, launcher pulse, live dots. All **disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`**. | Makes the store feel alive and polished, while staying accessible. |
| **Footer** | A pink **pennant** ("BOOLA BOOLA"), the patch logo, and quick category links. | Ends every page with brand personality and another path back into shopping. |
| **Mobile** | The hero stacks, the collage shrinks, the launcher becomes just the "ASK" patch, and nothing overflows sideways (checked at 375 px). | Most students shop on their phones. |
